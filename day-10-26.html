Splitting up and regrouping

  • The whole group walks together. Anyone who doesn't want to climb the basilica waits at a café on the square, and we meet at the basilica entrance at 15:50.
  • If the dinner starts at 18:00: skip the hotel after Heroes' Square and go straight to the venue. Take the 20E at 17:19 to Keleti, change to the M2 to Puskás Ferenc Stadion, then change to the 10E, arriving 17:45 (checked on Transitous 10-25).

Transport

  • Hotel → Fisherman's Bastion: tram 1, then M2 08:16 Puskás Ferenc Stadion → Batthyány tér 08:27, walk 13′, arrive 08:40 (checked on Transitous 10-22).
  • Castle District → Gravity: walk about 18′ down via the Vienna Gate; or bus 16A 10:18 Szentháromság tér → Széll Kálmán tér 10:21, walk 7′ (checked on Transitous 10-25).
  • Gravity → Horgásztanya: walk 5′ to Széll Kálmán tér, M2 12:44 → Batthyány tér 12:46, walk 8′, arrive 12:54; the next M2 is 12:49. Walking all the way takes 21′ (checked on Transitous 10-25).
  • Horgásztanya → St. Stephen's Basilica: walk about 23′, south along Fő utca and across the Chain Bridge (checked on Transitous 10-25).
  • If going to Mama Goulash instead: from Gravity walk 2′ to Széna tér, tram 4 or 6 at 12:39 → Király utca / Erzsébet körút 12:51, walk 10′, arrive 13:01; after the meal walk 12′ to the basilica, so leaving at 14:25 is fine. This way you skip the Chain Bridge (checked on Transitous 10-25).
  • Basilica → Heroes' Square: M1 15:59 Bajcsy-Zsilinszky út → Hősök tere 16:05, the next one is 16:02 (checked on Transitous 10-25).
  • Heroes' Square → hotel: M1 17:20 → Mexikói út 17:23, then tram 1 back (checked on Transitous 10-25).
  • Hotel → venue: tram 1 → Puskás Ferenc Stadion M, change to the 10E at 18:35 → Hungexpo, Kongresszusi Központ 18:45; or tram 37A 18:31 → Kőbánya felső 18:38, walk 11′ (checked on Transitous 10-25).
  • Venue → hotel (after the dinner): from the venue walk 10′ to Kőbánya felső, then tram 37A; or take the 10E to Puskás Ferenc Stadion and change to tram 1, about 19′ (searching from 22:00 gives two departures, 22:05 and 22:11; checked on Transitous 10-25). Walking all the way takes 41′, not recommended.
  • Tickets: the morning and midday trips are all covered by the 24-hour ticket bought on 10-25 (mine expires around 14:10; Companions' tickets expire at the time each of them first boarded on 10-25). After it expires, buy a second 24-hour ticket: I still need the M1, the ride back to the hotel, the ride to the venue, the ride back to the hotel after the dinner, and the ride to Keleti on the morning of 10-27. Paying per trip comes to about 2,800 Ft, while the second ticket is 2,750 Ft and you don't have to buy for every trip. Prices are under "Transport costs for this day" below.
