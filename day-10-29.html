Splitting up and regrouping

  • All day the 4 of us are together, no splitting (D-60).
  • If someone can't keep up: turn back together at Gosaulacke, or let the faster ones go on to the back lake while the rest wait at Gosaulacke (about 1 h 10 one way). I didn't check phone signal in the valley; agree on a turn-back time before splitting up.

Transport

School holidays (10-27 to 11-02) follow the winter timetable. The departures were checked on Transitous on 10-29 between 01:45 and 10:59 (Thursday, timetable, no real-time data); the 541 was about 9′ late in Bad Ischl (per the user).

Tickets: The 541 runs after 09:00, so the Freizeit-Ticket is valid. When boarding the 541, ask the driver for 2 ("Zwei Freizeit-Tickets, bitte"); one €26.50 ticket covers 2 adults, with unlimited rides from that day until 03:00 the next day, valid on the 541, 542, 543 and 544. Single tickets would cost about €20.80 per person (€10.40 there, the return estimated at the same price), about €83.20 for 4.
