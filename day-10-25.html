Splitting up and regrouping

  • I go bouldering alone in the afternoon, then meet my friends. You said not to worry about the companions' flights (decision D-46): the timeline leaves them a whole "Reserved" block; they can go to the hotel first to drop off luggage, or go straight to Retro Lángos / the pier.
  • Meeting point: the pier (Vigadó tér pier 5) for the Danube night cruise (booked and paid), check-in by 19:20 at the latest. If the companions have not reached the venue by 17:30, meet me at Retro Lángos (18:09) or at the pier by 19:20 at the latest.
  • Return: after getting off the boat, walk to the Chain Bridge, then head back to the hotel.

Transport

  • Daylight saving time ended early this morning, so all times below are winter time (CET).
  • Airport → MAG47 climbing gym (bouldering): 200E at 14:03 → Felsőcsatári út, change to bus 95 → Kada utca / Maglódi út at 14:32, then walk 3 minutes (checked on Transitous 10-22). If you miss it, take the 14:12 or 14:29 one.
  • MAG47 climbing gym (bouldering) → venue: walk 7′ + bus 85 + walk 19′, about 32 minutes (checked on Transitous 10-22).
  • Venue → next to Parliament (Kossuth Lajos tér): 10E + M2, 17:30 → 18:09 (checked on Transitous 10-22).
  • Chain Bridge → hotel: from the Chain Bridge walk 15′ to Deák Ferenc tér, then M3 → Népliget → tram 1 (checked on Transitous 10-22); the second plan arrives 22:20, and the last leg was not checked trip by trip.
  • Airport → hotel: 101E + tram 1 (checked on Transitous 10-22).
  • Re-check the services on BKK the day before leaving.
