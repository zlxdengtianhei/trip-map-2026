Fixed point

  1. Before 05:45: check in online in the Ryanair app (passport details required); after that you can only check in at the desk, for an extra fee.
  2. Document check (only for holders of non-EU/EEA passports): first go to the Ryanair Visa/Document check desk for a stamp, then go through security; without the stamp you cannot board and there is no refund. The desk opens at least 2 hours before departure and closes strictly at 07:05 (40 minutes before departure); some nationalities can do a "passport verification" during app check-in, and if it passes they do not need to go to the desk (Ryanair Help Centre, search snippet from 10-31).
  3. Gate closes 07:15 (old record, not rechecked); for the terminal go by the boarding pass in the Ryanair app (a third-party source says check-in at T1, security and boarding at T3, a 2021 statement).
  4. If you spend the night at the airport and miss the train, see the "Fixed point" section on 10-31.
