Fixed point

Today's and tomorrow morning's fixed points, in time order (D-75). 1. 17:30–17:45 sign up for the team competition: at the front desk of boulderbar Hannovergasse (the first boulderbar, 20th district). It is fine to miss this one: climb as usual or just watch. Whether signing up costs extra, and whether people who do not compete can still climb as usual, is not stated on the official events page; ask at the front desk. 2. Leave boulderbar Hannovergasse before 21:50: it closes at 22:00. Leave at 21:45, tram 12 + U4 + U1, and arrive at Hbf at 22:13. 3. Train to the airport (Wien Hbf, Vienna Central Station): - 22:42 Railjet (ÖBB express) → 22:57, recommended; - 22:53 REX (regional express) → 23:15; - 23:53 REX → 00:15, the last train tonight. All use the 24-hour ticket plus a €2.30 airport-section supplement (wien.info: people with a multi-day ticket only pay for the "airport → Vienna city limit" section; the same page says the same applies to Railjet). Train times checked on Transitous on 10-31 at 13:50. 4. If you miss all the trains: take the VAB 1 airport bus (Vienna Airport Lines) from Wien Hauptbahnhof Süd (the station on the south side of the Central Station); it runs all night: tonight 23:15 → 23:40, tomorrow morning 03:15 → 03:40 and 03:45 → 04:10 (checked on Transitous on 10-31 at 13:50). The bus does not accept Vienna city tickets; one way costs about €11 (third-party source, official price not found). The U1 metro runs all night on Saturday night. 5. Before 05:45 tomorrow morning: Ryanair online check-in: do it in the Ryanair app, passport details required; after 05:45 (2 hours before departure) you can only check in at the desk, for an extra fee. 6. Document check (only for holders of non-EU/EEA passports): first go to the Ryanair Visa/Document check desk for a stamp, then go through security; without the stamp you cannot board and there is no refund. The desk opens at least 2 hours before departure (no later than 05:45) and closes strictly at 07:05 (40 minutes before departure). Some nationalities can do a "passport verification" during app check-in, and if it passes they do not need to go to the desk. So the safest plan is to be at the desk before 05:45 tomorrow morning (Ryanair Help Centre, search snippet from 10-31; the help page itself would not open, so I did not read it directly). 7. Gate closes 07:15, flight departs 07:45: 07:15 is from an old record and was not rechecked. Terminal: a third-party source says Ryanair check-in is at T1 (Terminal 1) and security and boarding are at T3 (Terminal 3), which is a 2021 statement; go by the boarding pass in the Ryanair app. 8. Overnight at the airport: several third-party sites say the airport operates 24 hours with no curfew, and people staying overnight in public areas are generally not chased away; I found no clear statement on the airport's official site. Airport press release (2021–2022): on the 2nd floor of the T3 arrivals side (outside security) there are 16 Zzzleepandgo sleeping pods, €9/hour, from €48 for the whole night, booked at zzzleepandgo.com; the current price was not found. To spend nothing, find a bench in the T3 arrivals hall. 9. Food: INTERSPAR-pronto (small Spar supermarket) inside Hbf is open every day 06:00–23:00 (three third-party sources agree); after arriving at 22:13, buy dinner, water and tomorrow's breakfast here, as the shops at the airport may not be open at night.
