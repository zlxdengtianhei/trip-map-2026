Transport

  • Outbound: lodging → Bad Ischl station; bus 150 09:24 → Strobl 09:43, wait 16′, change to 546 09:59 → St. Wolfgang Markt 10:09 (checked on Transitous 10-23 for Wed 10-28; Thursday is the same). Not taking the 08:12 direct bus: it arrives at 08:38 and you would wait 2 h 27 min in St. Wolfgang.
  • Boat (official Herbstfahrplan PDF, checked against the chart on 10-22): St. Wolfgang Markt → Ried-Falkenstein → Fürberg → St. Gilgen: 11:05 → 11:20 → 11:37 → 11:45, 14:05 → 14:20 → 14:37 → 14:45, 17:00 → 17:20 → 17:37 → 17:45. These three sailings run whatever the weather; the 09:32, 12:35, 15:35 and 18:15 sailings are marked 'only after an announcement' in the timetable, so don't count on them. In the other direction (St. Gilgen → St. Wolfgang Markt) the sure ones are 09:20 → 10:00, 12:00 → 12:45, 15:00 → 15:45, 18:00 → 18:43 (this one ends its day at St. Wolfgang); the 10:20, 13:30 and 16:30 wait for an announcement.
  • One-way fares (official price list from 2026-02-01, the table is the 2025-09 edition): Markt → Fürberg €11.80, → St. Gilgen €11.80; Fürberg → St. Gilgen €6.00. No student price.
  • Cable car: Zwölferhorn 4/1–11/1 daily 09:00–17:00, closed for maintenance 11/2–11/19; round trip adult €36, student and youth discount €32 (show ID; whether ISIC is accepted is unchecked). The official site doesn't say when the last descent is; be down before 16:00. From the summit walk 10′ to the cross for the view of the lake.
  • How far the valley station is from the St. Gilgen bus stop: the sources say 5′, 1.25 km and 32′; I estimate 15′, check the map when you get there.
  • Return: bus 150 St. Gilgen 18:34 → Bad Ischl 19:08 (checked on Transitous 10-23); every 30′ (at :04 and :34; there is also an EILKURS at 18:10 → 18:37). Rainy-day version 13:08 → 13:45, 13:38 → 14:15, 14:38 → 15:15, 15:08 → 15:45. Bus 546 direct from St. Wolfgang to Bad Ischl: 12:32, 13:15, 15:15, 17:15 (32′). K³ is a 26′ walk from the train station (Transitous).
  • Re-check the services on ÖBB Scotty / OÖVV the day before leaving.
